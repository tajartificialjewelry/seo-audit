Tools

Six tools, one workspace. Everything runs from your account.

Ranking plan

Turns your site and target keywords into a ready-to-use fix list.

  • A new title and meta description for every page
  • Internal links to add between your own pages
  • New pages to publish for keywords your site does not cover
  • Downloadable sitemap.xml, robots.txt and schema markup

Site audit

Crawls your pages and shows what is holding the site back.

  • A health score with serious, medium and minor issues
  • Broken link check and site-wide checks: robots.txt, sitemap, HTTPS, soft 404
  • Speed, page size, noindex and thin-content warnings
  • Download the report as CSV or print it

Keyword research

Finds what people really type into search.

  • Hundreds of keyword ideas from one seed word
  • Question keywords such as how, what and why
  • Copy the best ones straight into your ranking plan

Competitor gap

Compares your site with up to three competitors.

  • Terms competitors use that you do not
  • The headings competitors publish
  • Ideas for pages you are missing

Article outlines

Gives every new page a clear structure before you write.

  • Title, H1 and meta description
  • H2 sections built from real questions
  • FAQ and related keywords to include

Rank tracker

Saves where your site appears for your keywords.

  • Check up to 5 keywords per run
  • History saved in your browser
  • Estimates based on DuckDuckGo results
