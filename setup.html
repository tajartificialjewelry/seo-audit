Connect your backend

Do this once. Paste your Google Apps Script Web app URL, test it, then download a ready-made config.js and replace the old file in your GitHub repo.

After downloading

  1. Open your GitHub repo, click config.js, then the pencil icon (or Add file > Upload files to replace it).
  2. Paste or upload the new content and commit.
  3. Wait 1 to 2 minutes, then press Ctrl+F5 on your site.
